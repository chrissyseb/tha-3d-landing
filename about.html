A typical tutor vs an Exam Preparation Specialist™

A typical tutor starts from the school worksheet and covers topics. An exam is one paper, with its own format and marking scheme. A Specialist starts from that paper, sets timed papers in the real format and works on where marks are lost.

How matching works

From your enquiry to the first lesson

  1. Tell us the exam

    The level, the subjects and your WhatsApp number. It takes under a minute.

  2. We reply within one working day

    We reply on WhatsApp and match your child with a Specialist for that paper, usually within 48 hours.

  3. You talk to the Specialist

    You discuss the plan to exam day. Lesson fees and the schedule are agreed with you before any lesson.

  4. Lessons start

    We stay involved through the first month. If the fit is wrong, tell us and we’ll re-match you with another Specialist for free.

Who the Specialists are

Every Exam Preparation Specialist™ applies to us, and we decide who teaches.

  • Reviewed before they teach We read every application ourselves. We may ask for documents or a short call before we decide.
  • Briefed on your child’s paper Before the first lesson, your Specialist gets our Exam Brief™: the current syllabus, the paper format, how the marks are given and where students usually lose them.
  • Held to our Specialist terms Specialists agree to behave professionally, to put safeguarding first and to keep parent and student information confidential.
  • Independent educators Specialists are not our employees. Parents pay them directly, and we invoice the Specialist a one-off placement fee on the first month of lessons.

The full rules are in the Specialist terms.
