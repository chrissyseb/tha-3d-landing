What is inside a pack

A pack is built from up to four parts. Each card below lists what that pack holds.

  1. How the paper is set

    The format, how it is marked and where students lose marks.

  2. The method

    One routine or answer frame to follow, such as the 5-step BUG routine in PSLE Comprehension.

  3. Practice with full answer keys

    Original questions, each with its answer and the mark rationale behind it.

  4. A timed mock

    One paper to sit against the clock.
