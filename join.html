Who we're looking for

Educators with real experience of a particular paper: current or former school teachers, centre teachers and experienced independent educators who know the syllabus, the question types and the mark scheme.

What you get

  • Students matched to your exact paper We bring you families whose exam, level and subject fit what you teach. You accept or decline each match.
  • An Exam Brief™ for every match Before the first lesson you get our brief on the child’s paper: the current syllabus, the format, how the marks are given and where students lose them.
  • No chasing assignments No bidding on job posts. We do the matching and stay involved through the first month.
  • Free resources for your students Your students can use our free test papers and EssayLab™’s free tier.
  • Your rates, your plan You set your own rates and agree the plan directly with each family.

What it costs

No joining fee and no subscription. Families pay you directly for lessons. For each student we place with you, we invoice a one-off placement fee of 50% of the agreed first-month lesson value. Nothing from later months.

How it works for Specialists

From application to your first matched student

  1. Apply

    Tell us what you teach, which exam papers you know, your experience and your usual rate. It takes about ten minutes.

  2. We review your application

    We read every application ourselves. We may ask for documents, such as proof of qualifications or past teaching, or a short call before we decide.

  3. Approved Specialists are offered matched students

    When a family's exam, level, format and schedule fit what you teach, we offer you the match. You can accept or decline.

  4. Agree the plan and fees with the family

    You and the family agree the lesson plan, schedule and lesson fees before anything starts.

  5. Placement fee on the first month only

    The family pays you directly. We invoice you a placement fee of 50% of the agreed first-month lesson value (lessons × the rate you agreed with the family), payable within 7 days. From the second month on, there is no fee.

"Approved" means your application has been reviewed and accepted by Thinking Hack. We only describe a Specialist as "verified" once we have actually checked their documents. Applying, or being approved, does not guarantee any placements: matches depend on what families ask for.

The placement fee

50% of the first month. Nothing after that.

  • No joining fee.
  • No subscription or listing fee.
  • Families pay you directly for lessons.
  • A placement fee of 50% of the agreed first-month lesson value (lessons × rate), invoiced to you for each student placed.
  • Nothing from later months.

The full rules, including cancellations, refunds and what happens if a student stops early, are in the Specialist terms.

Worked example

A family books 4 lessons in the first month at S$80 per lesson.

First-month lesson value (4 × S$80), paid to you by the familyS$320
Placement fee invoiced by Thinking Hack (50%)S$160
You keep for the first monthS$160
Later months100% to you

The invoice is payable within 7 days, by the Stripe payment link we send or another method we agree. If first-month lessons are cancelled or the student stops, the fee is recalculated on lessons actually delivered. Later months are paid to you in full by the family. The figures above are an example only; you set your own rate.
