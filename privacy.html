Privacy notice

How we handle your personal data

This notice explains what personal data Thinking Hack™ collects through this website and over WhatsApp, why we collect it, who sees it, how long we keep it and how you can ask us to access, correct or delete it. We aim to handle personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

Last updated 17 October 2026. Thinking Hack is in the process of formal business registration; this notice will be updated with the registered business name when that is complete.

Who we are

Thinking Hack is an exam-preparation agency in Singapore. We match students with Exam Preparation Specialists™ and run Thinking Hack Masterclasses™ and Thinking Hack Exam Notes™. In this notice, "we", "us" and "our" mean Thinking Hack.

What we collect

When you enquire about a Specialist or a masterclass

  • The name of the parent, guardian or student making the enquiry
  • Email address and WhatsApp number
  • The student's level and, if you choose to give it, their school
  • Subjects, current grades, goals and the difficulties the student is having
  • Preferred lesson format (online or in person), area, days and times, and budget
  • Anything else you write in your message

When you apply to become an Exam Preparation Specialist™

  • Name, email address and WhatsApp number
  • Subjects and levels you teach, qualifications and teaching experience
  • Lesson formats, areas, availability and rates
  • Links you provide to credentials or past work, and what you tell us about yourself

When you book or buy

Payments are processed by Stripe on its secure checkout page. We do not see or store card numbers. We keep an order record with your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, the student's name and level, the item you booked or bought, the amount and the payment status.

When you message or speak with us

We keep the messages you send us on WhatsApp or by email, and notes of what we agreed, so we can follow up properly.

We only ask for what we need. Please do not send us sensitive information we have not asked for, such as NRIC numbers, medical records or full report books.

Why we use it

  • To respond to you: to reply to your enquiry or application and answer your questions.
  • To match: to choose a Specialist whose subjects, level, format, area and schedule fit the student.
  • To arrange lessons and masterclasses: to introduce the parent and Specialist, confirm schedules, reserve masterclass places, take payment for bookings and invoice placement fees.
  • To review Specialist applications: to check experience and credentials and decide whether to offer a placement.
  • To run and improve the service: to keep records of what was agreed, resolve problems and understand, in aggregate, how the website is used.

By submitting a form or messaging us, you consent to us using your personal data for these purposes. We will ask again before using it for anything materially different.

Who sees it

  • Thinking Hack: only the people at Thinking Hack who handle enquiries and applications.
  • A shortlisted Specialist: when we propose a Specialist, we describe the student's learning needs to them. The Specialist sees the student's learning details and the parent's contact details only after the parent agrees to go ahead with that Specialist.
  • Parents, about Specialists: if we propose you as a Specialist, we share a summary of your subjects, experience and availability with that parent. We share your contact details only after you agree to the placement.
  • Service providers: companies that help us run the service process data on our behalf, for example Vercel, which hosts this website and stores form submissions in Vercel's cloud storage, Stripe, which processes payments, an email delivery provider that sends us (Thinking Hack) notifications about new enquiries, applications and payments, and WhatsApp or email providers when we reply to you. Our notification emails contain only the type of record, a reference and a link to our admin page. Some of these providers may store or process data outside Singapore.
  • When required by law: if we are legally required to disclose it.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with other businesses for their marketing.

Children's data

Most of the students we help are under 18. We expect a parent or guardian to make the enquiry, or to agree to it, and by submitting details about a child you confirm that you are their parent or guardian or have their permission. If you are a student under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sending us your details. If we learn that we hold a child's details without a parent's or guardian's agreement, we will contact the family or delete them.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: deleted within 24 months of our last contact with you, unless a placement is still ongoing, in which case we keep them while the placement continues and delete them within 24 months after it ends.
  • Specialist applications: kept for 24 months so we can consider you for future students, then deleted, unless you are working with us on an active placement.

We may keep a limited record for longer where the law requires it, for example records of payments.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it has been used;
  • correct personal data that is wrong or out of date;
  • stop using your personal data (withdraw your consent); or
  • delete your personal data.

Send your request on WhatsApp to +65 9003 0002. We may ask you to confirm who you are before acting on it, and we aim to reply within 30 days. If you withdraw consent, we may no longer be able to match you, continue a placement or consider your application, and we will tell you what that means before we act.

How we protect it

  • Form submissions are kept in private storage that is not publicly accessible.
  • They can only be viewed through an admin page protected by a secret key, used only by Thinking Hack.
  • This website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

No method of storing or sending data online is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you share with us. If we become aware of a data breach that affects you, we will tell you and, where required, the authorities.

Website analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to see aggregate page statistics, such as how many people visited a page and which pages are popular. It does not use cookies and is not used to identify individual visitors. We do not use advertising trackers on this website.

EssayLab™

EssayLab™ is a separate service at essay-marker-umber.vercel.app, with its own terms and privacy information. This notice covers this website only. Please read EssayLab's own information before using it or buying an EssayLab Pass there.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when our business details or the way we handle data changes, and change the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use data you have already given us, we will tell you.

Contact
