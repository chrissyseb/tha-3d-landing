Privacy notice

VocaBobby is run by Thinking Hack Education, Singapore. We collect as little as possible, especially about children.

What we keep

Parents: email address, a scrambled (hashed) password, purchase records. Players: a nickname you choose, a scrambled PIN, and game progress (words practised, scores, coins, avatar). We do not ask for a child's real name, age, school, photo or location.

Why

To let players log in on any device, save progress, show the leaderboard (nicknames only) and give parents a progress report. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store card details.

Who sees it

Other players see only nicknames, avatar, world and scores on the leaderboard. We don't sell data or show ads. Data is stored with our hosting provider (Vercel).

Your choices

Parents can ask us to correct or delete their family's data at any time: WhatsApp +65 9003 0002. Guest play keeps progress only on your own device.

This notice follows the spirit of Singapore's PDPA. Last updated 27 Nov 2026.
