VocaBobby

For teachers and schools

VocaBobby is a free, browser-based English vocabulary game for Primary and Secondary students aged 9 to 15, made in Singapore by Thinking Hack Education. Every move in the game is a vocabulary question.

Educational purpose

  • Meanings in context: each word is taught with a short British English example sentence.
  • Spaced review: words come back after hours, then days, then weeks, until they stick.
  • Spelling and pronunciation: hear-it-spell-it practice and listen-and-say-it practice.
  • Look-alike words (affect and effect, stationary and stationery) are practised side by side.
  • Your own lists: teachers can turn a spelling list or a chapter of text into a playable Quest world and share it with a 6-letter class code.

Safety

  • No chat. Players cannot send messages to each other.
  • No ads and no third-party ad trackers.
  • Minimal data. Children play as a guest, or with a nickname and a 4-digit PIN. We never ask for a child's real name, age, school, photo or location.
  • No comparing children. Boards show effort only, and each child gets their own questions.
  • Nothing for children to buy. Rewards are cosmetic, and children are never asked for money.

How to allowlist the site

School-managed devices (including Singapore MOE devices) may block new sites by default. Please ask your school IT team or device administrator to allow:

  • vocabobby.vercel.app (the game and its data, served over HTTPS)
  • fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com (fonts; the game still works if these stay blocked)

Suggested category: Education. The site uses no pop-ups, no downloads and no third-party logins. Microphone access is optional and only used, with permission, for say-it pronunciation practice.
