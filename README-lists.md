# My Lists (custom Quests) and the self-growing word bank

## How a list is built
Every word typed or ticked is built automatically on the server (`api/lists.js`) from `api/_dict.js`, a compact bundle (about 2 MB, 13,000 headwords plus 9,700 inflection redirects) generated by `build_dict.py`:

| Field | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning + part of speech | Open English WordNet 2024 (https://en-word.net) | CC BY 4.0 |
| Example sentence with a blank | Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), English side of the manythings.org fra-eng export; sentence ids kept in the bundle | CC BY 2.0 FR |
| Level | CEFR-J Vocabulary Profile 1.5 (Tono Laboratory, TUFS) and Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2 1.0 | cited / CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| Fallback level (unlisted words) | wordfreq Zipf frequency | Apache 2.0 / CC BY-SA 4.0 data |
| Syllables and stress | pyphen (en_GB hyphenation patterns) + CMU Pronouncing Dictionary stress | LGPL/MPL, BSD |
| Inflection redirects (caught → catch) | lemminflect, WordNet Morphy | MIT, CC BY 4.0 |
| Distractors | 3 words from the bundle at the same level and part of speech | n/a |
| Spelling tip | rules: -ible, i before e after c, silent letters, double letters | n/a |

Sentences are filtered for child safety (violence, alcohol, romance and similar words removed) and kept to 5–14 words.

Confidence: 2 = level-listed word with a WordNet meaning, 1 = meaning but unlisted or no example, 0 = no meaning. Confidence-0 words are marked "needs a meaning" in the builder for the owner to type. Spot-check on 40 random common words: 3 needed a manual meaning (haha, although, cannot).

## Rules
- Up to 100 words per list. Free accounts: 1 list. Family or PSLE Pass: up to 20. Guests: 1 list kept on the device only.
- Names, themes, words and typed meanings pass the blocklist (`cleanOk` in `api/_lib.js`, extends the nickname blocklist).
- Lists are private (owner's family only) unless "Share with a code" is ticked. A list is only playable with its 6-character code; there is no public browse.

## Self-growing bank
High-confidence, non-blocked words that are not in `words.json` are recorded as candidates (`candidates/<word>.json`). When a candidate appears in 3 different lists it is promoted into `bank/auto.json` at its CEFR level and loaded into the game's main pool. Every candidate, promotion and removal is logged in `bank/log.json`. Admin (`/admin.html` → Word bank: auto-added) shows promoted words with a Remove button; removed words never come back.

## Optional LLM polish (OFF)
Set both env vars in Vercel to turn it on: `LIST_LLM_POLISH=1` and `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=<key>` (optional `LIST_LLM_MODEL`). Meanings are then rewritten into simple British English for a 10-year-old. With either missing, nothing is called and lists stay fully automatic and free.

## Rebuilding the data
`python3 build_dict.py api/_dict.js` (needs `wn` with `oewn:2024`, `pyphen`, `pronouncing`, `wordfreq`, `lemminflect`, the OLP CSVs and the Tatoeba fra-eng file).
