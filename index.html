Methods

How I teach

Every subject is taught as a system with a name, so students know exactly what to do when they open the paper.

PSLE English

The BUG Method

Box the keywords, underline the context clues, glance back. A fixed routine for decoding comprehension traps, paired with grammar templates for synthesis and transformation.

PSLE Mathematics

Model Drawing into Heuristics

Visual models first, then the shortcuts: unitary method, constant difference, working backwards. Problem sums are scaffolded step by step so method marks are never lost.

O-Level English

PEEL Essay Architecture

Point, evidence, explanation, link. Plus systematic decoding for visual texts, narrative and non-narrative comprehension, and drills that remove recurring grammar slips under time pressure.

O-Level Humanities

The SBCS Skill Toolkit

Every source-based question type covered: comparison, cross-referencing, surprise, purpose and utility. Plus causal factor mapping for structured essays, and case study banks for Geography.

O-Level Literature

PEEL-L

Close reading built around literary device analysis and thematic linkage, with a three-step strategy for unseen poetry and prose.

A-Level General Paper

AQ Localisation + Macro-Argumentation

Global arguments evaluated through Singapore's socio-cultural lens, with thesis refinement and anticipated counter-perspectives for top-band evaluation marks.
