    Questions parents ask

    Are you a tutor or a tuition agency?

    We are not tutors. We are Exam Preparation Specialists™. Tell us the exam, and we match your child with a Specialist for that exact paper, who plans every lesson around how it is marked. Matching is free for parents.

    Why be matched instead of choosing a specialist ourselves?

    Because the right fit depends on the exact paper. We shortlist an Exam Preparation Specialist™ on the specific exam, the student's current and target grades, how they like to learn and when they are free. You still decide: you talk to the Specialist about the plan before committing, and if you have a preference, tell us in the form.

    Are lessons at home or online?

    Either. 1:1 lessons are with a matched Specialist, at home or online, depending on who is available for the exam and area. Tell us what you prefer in the form.

    How much does it cost?

    Enquiring and matching are free for parents. For 1:1 lessons, our regular hourly rates are listed in the Rates section. Lesson fees are agreed with you before lessons start.

    The exam is coming up soon. Is it too late?

    No. In the final weeks we switch to full timed papers and quick marking, and work on the marks still on the table.

    What if it isn't the right fit?

    Tell us and we'll match you with another Specialist.

    How is an Exam Preparation Specialist™ different?

    A Specialist helps a student understand the subject, then trains them to score: how the paper is set, how it is marked, how to manage time, and how to avoid losing marks they already know how to get.

    What happens to my details?

    We use them only to handle your request and arrange a match. A shortlisted Specialist sees the student's learning details, and your contact details only after you agree. Read the privacy notice.

    Do you have a tuition centre?

    No. Lessons are 1:1 at your home or online, with a Specialist matched to your child’s exact paper. We cover all areas of Singapore.

    Can we switch Specialists?

    Yes. Not the right fit? We’ll re-match you with another Specialist for free.

    Can we reschedule?

    Yes, with at least 24 hours’ notice to your Specialist. A lesson cancelled with less notice may be charged at 50% of its fee.

    Is there a free trial?

    No. Matching is free, and the first lesson is a normal paid lesson. If the fit isn’t right, we re-match you for free.

    How do we pay?

    You pay your Specialist directly, only for lessons held. Fees and how you pay are agreed before the first lesson. There’s no matching fee for parents.

    Is there a contract?

    No. Stop whenever you like.

    Why one rate per level?

    Every Specialist is chosen for the exam your child is sitting, so there is no tier to pick. You pay the regular rate for the level.

    How many lessons a week?

    Most students start with one 1.5-hour lesson a week. In the months before the exam, some add a second for timed papers.

    When should we start?

    For PSLE, many families start in P5 or the November before P6. For O-Level, Sec 3. For A-Level, JC1. Starting later still helps, and the plan just gets tighter.

    How are Specialists checked?

    Every Specialist applies and is reviewed by us before teaching. We can ask for proof of qualifications or past teaching, or a short call.
