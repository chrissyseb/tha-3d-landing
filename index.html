What is VocaBobby?

VocaBobby is an educational vocabulary game for Primary and Secondary school students in Singapore. Every jump in the game is a vocabulary question: word meanings, words in context, spelling and pronunciation. Words a child finds tricky come back on a spaced-review schedule until they stick.

How it helps children learn

  • Meanings taught in a short example sentence, in British English
  • Spaced review: each word returns after hours, then days, then weeks
  • Spelling practice with hints for tricky letter patterns
  • Listen and say-it practice for pronunciation
  • Look-alike words (affect and effect, stationary and stationery) practised side by side

For parents

A free parent dashboard shows a weekly summary for each child: words met, words learnt, tricky words and what to practise next, plus dinner-table questions using the week's words. Children play with a nickname and a 4-digit PIN only. There are no ads and no chat.

For teachers and schools

VocaBobby can be used in class or for homework revision, and teachers can turn any word list into a playable world. Information for teachers and school IT teams, including how to allowlist the site.
