Specialist terms

How Thinking Hack™ works with Exam Preparation Specialists™: what we do, the placement fee, how payment works and what happens when plans change.

1. Who these terms are for

These terms apply to educators who apply to, or are approved by, Thinking Hack to be offered students as an Exam Preparation Specialist™ ("Specialist", "you"). "Thinking Hack", "we" and "us" means Thinking Hack. "Family" means the parent, guardian or adult student who asks us for a match.

By ticking the box on the application form you confirm you have read these terms and agree to them for any student we place with you.

2. What Thinking Hack does

  • We introduce you to families whose exam, level, subject, format and schedule fit what you teach.
  • We match carefully, but you are free to accept or decline any match we offer.
  • We stay involved through the first month of each placement: checking in with you and the family, and helping if anything isn't working.

You are an independent educator, not an employee, agent or partner of Thinking Hack. You decide how you teach, set your own rates and are responsible for your own tax, CPF (if applicable) and any insurance. Nothing in these terms creates an employment relationship.

3. The placement fee

The family pays you directly for lessons. Thinking Hack does not collect lesson fees from families and does not pay you for lessons.

For each student we place with you, Thinking Hack invoices you a one-off placement fee. It is 50% of the agreed first-month lesson value: the number of lessons in the first month multiplied by the rate you agreed with the family. A different rate applies only if we agree it with you in writing for that placement.

"First month" means the first calendar month of lessons, or the first 4 weeks starting from the first lesson, whichever is agreed with the family when the placement is confirmed. If nothing is agreed, it is the first 4 weeks from the first lesson.

There is no joining fee, no subscription and no fee for later months. If first-month lessons are cancelled or the student stops, the fee is recalculated on the lessons actually delivered (see sections 6 and 7).

4. How payment works

  1. Before the first lesson, you and the family agree the number of first-month lessons and your rate, and you tell us what was agreed.
  2. The family pays you directly for lessons, under the arrangement you agree with them.
  3. Thinking Hack sends you an invoice for the placement fee.
  4. You pay the invoice within 7 days, by the Stripe payment link we send you, or by another method we agree with you (for example, bank transfer).

Card payments through the Stripe link are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your card number.

5. Cancellations and rescheduling

  • Either side should give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a lesson.
  • A first-month lesson that is rescheduled and still delivered within the first month counts as a first-month lesson.
  • Late cancellations by the family (less than 24 hours' notice) follow your own cancellation policy, which you must share with the family in writing before the first lesson. If your policy says a late-cancelled lesson is still charged and the family pays for it, it counts as a delivered lesson for the placement fee.
  • If you cancel a lesson and cannot reschedule it within the first month, it should be refunded to the family, or moved to the second month by agreement.

6. Refunds and adjustments

If first-month lessons are cancelled and not delivered, the placement fee is recalculated on the lessons actually delivered. If you have already paid more than the recalculated fee, we refund the difference to you or, if you prefer, credit it against a future placement fee. Any refund you give the family for lessons is between you and the family.

7. If a student stops or a match fails

  • Student stops during the first month: the placement fee is recalculated on the lessons actually delivered, and any overpayment is refunded or credited as in section 6.
  • The match isn't working: if the family or you tell us the match isn't right, we may introduce the family to a replacement Specialist. The placement fee is never charged twice for the same month: each Specialist's fee is based only on the first-month lessons they actually delivered.

8. Later months

After the first month there is no placement fee. Lessons, fees and payment are arranged directly between you and the family, unless we agree something different with you in writing.

9. Non-circumvention

If, within 12 months of our introducing a family to you, you teach that family (including another child in the same family) outside these terms, for example by arranging lessons directly to avoid the placement fee, the placement fee is still payable. It is worked out as 50% of the first-month lesson value for those lessons, as if the placement had gone through Thinking Hack.

10. Application, review and status

Your application can have one of these statuses:

  • New application: received, not yet reviewed.
  • Under review: we are reading your application, and may ask for documents or a short call.
  • Approved: Thinking Hack has reviewed your application and is willing to offer you matched students.
  • Rejected: we are not able to offer you students at this time.
  • Inactive: you or we have paused or ended the arrangement.

"Approved" means reviewed by Thinking Hack. We will only describe you as "verified", or describe a qualification as verified, once we have actually checked the relevant documents. Being approved does not guarantee any placements; matches depend on what families ask for.

You must tell us promptly if anything in your application stops being true.

11. Conduct, child safety and data

  • Behave professionally with students and families at all times. Many students are children: safeguarding comes first. For lessons with a child, a parent or guardian should know when and where lessons take place.
  • Tell us straight away about any safeguarding concern involving a student we placed with you.
  • Keep family and student information confidential. Use it only for the placement it was shared for, and handle it in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).
  • Do not contact families we have told you about except for students actually placed with you, and do not pass their details to anyone else.
  • We handle your application details as described in our privacy notice.

12. Ending the arrangement

You can stop receiving matches at any time by telling us. We can stop offering you matches, or end the arrangement, by telling you, for example if a family raises a serious concern or these terms are not followed. Ending the arrangement does not cancel any placement fee already due, the non-circumvention clause (section 9) for introductions already made, or your obligations on confidentiality and data.

If you stop teaching a placed student, please give the family reasonable notice and tell us, so we can help them find another Specialist.

13. Changes and contact

These terms are a draft and may change. If we change them, we will post the new version on this page with a new version date and tell approved Specialists before the change applies to new placements. Placements already confirmed stay on the terms agreed at the time, unless we both agree otherwise in writing.
