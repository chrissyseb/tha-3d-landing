How Accurate Is the Score?

Every essay is marked against its paper's published SEAB, Cambridge or IB criteria, criterion by criterion. The score is an estimate for that one paper, to guide revision. It isn't a prediction of your overall exam grade, and your school's or examiner's mark may differ. EssayLabsg™ is not affiliated with or endorsed by SEAB, Cambridge Assessment or the IB.
