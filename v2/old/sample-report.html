GP Paper 1 · Detailed report‘Social media does more harm than good to young people.’ How far do you agree?
28/ 50Est. grade C
Comments on every paragraph

In today’s modern world, social media is everywhere. Some people think that social media does more harm than good, while others disagree.Define “harm” and set your yardstick here.

Firstly, social media affects the mental health of young people. Many studies have shown that teenagers who use social media more are more unhappy.Which studies? Name one, with a figure.

However, some people may argue that social media is good. But this benefit is small compared to the harms.Weigh it, don’t wave it away. PEEL!

Every criterion, in full
Content16/30

A clear stand, but the argument stays general. Examples are unnamed, and the counter-argument is dismissed instead of weighed.

Language12/20

Generally clear and accurate. Repetitive openers (“Firstly”, “However”) and a narrow range of vocabulary hold it back.

Top fixes, with a rewrite
  1. Set a yardstick in the introductionSome people think that social media does more harm than good.Judged by its effect on young people’s well-being and attention, social media does more harm than good, though not for every user.
  2. Make every example checkableName the study, the year and the finding, then link it back to the question.
  3. Weigh the other side before you rebut itConcede the real benefit first, then show why it is outweighed on your yardstick.
